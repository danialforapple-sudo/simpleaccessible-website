Services

From a one-off audit to embedding accessibility in how your team works.

  • Accessibility audits

    Expert manual and assistive-technology testing against WCAG 2.2 AA, with a prioritised report your developers can act on.

  • Remediation support

    Hands-on help fixing issues: code reviews, pairing sessions and re-testing until barriers are gone.

  • Inclusive design reviews

    Catch problems before they're built. We review designs, prototypes and design systems for accessibility.

  • Training & workshops

    Role-based training for developers, designers, content editors and product owners — remote or on site.

  • User testing with disabled people

    Research sessions with screen reader, magnification, voice control and keyboard-only users.

  • Compliance & statements

    Accessibility statements, VPATs / ACRs and guidance on the European Accessibility Act and procurement.

Selected work

A few projects where accessibility made a measurable difference.

  • E-commerce · Audit & remediation

    Making checkout usable for screen reader users

    A national retailer's checkout was blocking keyboard and screen reader users. We audited the full purchase flow and paired with the team on fixes.

    Result: 94% fewer critical issues and a 12% lift in checkout completion.

  • Public sector · Training

    Accessibility training for a municipal digital team

    A six-week programme for 60 developers, designers and editors, tailored to their CMS and component library.

    Result: New pages now pass WCAG 2.2 AA at launch.

  • SaaS · Design system review

    An accessible foundation for a B2B design system

    We reviewed 45 components, rewrote interaction patterns and documented accessibility guidance for each one.

    Result: Every product built on the system inherits accessible components.

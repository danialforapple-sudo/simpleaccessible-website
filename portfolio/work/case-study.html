Making checkout usable for screen reader users

A national retailer was losing customers at the final step. We found out why — and helped fix it.

Client
National retailer
Services
Audit, remediation support
Duration
10 weeks
Standard
WCAG 2.2 AA

The challenge

Customer support was receiving complaints from blind and low-vision customers who couldn't complete purchases. The checkout had been rebuilt in a JavaScript framework without accessibility testing.

What we did

  • Tested the full purchase journey with NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver and keyboard only.
  • Logged 87 issues, each with severity, affected users and a code-level fix.
  • Ran weekly pairing sessions with the front-end team.
  • Ran usability sessions with five screen reader users to validate the fixes.

Key barriers found

  • Custom address dropdown couldn't be operated by keyboard.
  • Form errors were shown only in red, with no text announcement.
  • Payment iframe trapped keyboard focus.
  • Session timeout gave no warning or way to extend.

Results

  • 94% reduction in critical issues.
  • 12% increase in checkout completion across all users.
  • Accessibility checks added to the team's pull request template.
